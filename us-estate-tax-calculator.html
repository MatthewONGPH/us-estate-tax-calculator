The NRA unified credit is USD 13,000, which offsets tax on the first USD 60,000. For US citizens and domiciliaries, the exemption is USD 13.61M (2024) — expected to drop to approximately USD 7M when the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act sunsets at end of 2025.

The Irish ETF alternative: UCITS ETFs domiciled in Ireland (e.g. IWDA, CSPX, VWRA on the London Stock Exchange) track the same indices as US ETFs but are not US-situs assets. Singaporean investors holding Irish-domiciled equivalents avoid US estate tax exposure entirely. Verify suitability with a financial adviser.
No treaty protection: Countries like the UK, Germany, and France have US estate tax treaties raising their citizens' US-situs exemption to USD 11.18M+. Singapore has no such treaty. Singaporeans receive only the USD 60,000 NRA exemption.
